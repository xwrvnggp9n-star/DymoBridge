DymoBridge

This installs DymoBridge, which lets browser apps (such as Kipu) print labels to the DYMO LabelWriter on this Mac.

It replaces DYMO’s own web service (which is turned off, reversibly) and starts automatically in the background — nothing to open or configure after installation.

Before you continue: DYMO Connect for Desktop should already be installed and the LabelWriter plugged in, so the printer appears in System Settings → Printers & Scanners.
